Point and mark

The cursor is point. C-SPC sets the mark there, and the text between point and the mark is the region. While the mark is active the region is highlighted and the region commands act on it (transient_mark_mode); Shift with a motion key selects too. Typing inserts at point even with an active region unless delete_selection_mode is true; DEL and C-d delete an active region either way.

keyscommandwhat it does
C-SPCset-mark-commandset the mark at point and activate it
C-x C-xexchange-point-and-markexchange point and the mark
C-x hmark-whole-buffermark the whole buffer as the region

Killing and yanking

Killing is cutting and yanking is pasting. The kills are kept for yanking (kill_ring_max of them), and every kill also goes to the Windows clipboard; C-y takes the clipboard when another program changed it since.

keyscommandwhat it does
C-w M-wkill-region kill-ring-savecut or copy the region
C-kkill-linekill to the end of the line
M-d M-DELkill-word backward-kill-wordkill a word forward or back
C-S-DELkill-whole-linekill the whole line, its newline included
C-yyankpaste the last kill
M-yyank-popright after a yank, replace it with an older kill

Undo

C-/ undoes, and repeated it goes further back; after any other command it first undoes the undos, as in Emacs. C-? (undo-redo) goes forward again. The history kept per buffer is undo_limit_mb megabytes: the oldest changes are dropped first, and the last command can always be undone.

keyscommandwhat it does
C-/ C-x uundoundo
C-?undo-redogo forward again

Searching

isearch highlights every match on screen, follows smart case (a capital letter makes it exact) and wraps around.

keyscommandwhat it does
C-s C-risearch-forward isearch-backwardsearch forward / backward as you type

While isearch runs:

keyscommandwhat it does
C-s C-risearch-repeat-forward isearch-repeat-backwardthe next / previous match
C-wisearch-yank-wordadd the next word at point to the search
C-yisearch-yank-killadd the last kill to the search
M-p M-nisearch-ring-retreat isearch-ring-advancethe previous / next search string of the history
M-cisearch-toggle-case-foldswitch between exact case and either case
RETisearch-exitstay at the match (the mark is left where the search started)
C-gisearch-abortgo back to where the search started

Replacing

M-% replaces from point (or in the region), asking at each match: y or SPC replace, n or DEL skip, ! replaces all the rest, . replaces this one and stops, q or RET stop, C-g stops too. M-x replace-string replaces without asking. A lowercase replacement of a lowercase search string follows the case of each match (Foo becomes Bar, FOO becomes BAR). One undo takes back a whole session.

keyscommandwhat it does
M-%query-replacereplace from point, asking at each match

Indentation

RET indents the new line, TAB reindents the line (or the region), and a closing ), ] or } typed first on a line reindents it. indent_width sets the columns per level and indent_with_tabs tabs or spaces; with detect_indentation a loaded file decides tabs or spaces for its buffer from its first indented lines.

keyscommandwhat it does
TABindent-for-tab-commandreindent the line, or the region
<backtab>unindentremove one level of indentation from the line or region
M-itab-to-tab-stopinsert whitespace up to the next tab stop
C-qquoted-insertinsert the next character typed literally

Multiple cursors

The commands of Emacs' multiple-cursors package, without its mc/ prefix. Typing, motions, kills, yanks and indentation then act at every cursor, in one undo step; C-g first deactivates their regions, then leaves one cursor. Ctrl+Shift+click adds a cursor or removes the one there. A command that would add more than max_cursors cursors stops there and says so.

keyscommandwhat it does
C-> C-<mark-next-like-this mark-previous-like-thisa cursor at the next / previous occurrence of the region, or on the next / previous line
C-c C-<mark-all-like-thisa cursor at every occurrence of the region
C-S-c C-S-cedit-linesa cursor on every line of the region

M-x skip-to-next-like-this, M-x unmark-next-like-this, M-x edit-ends-of-lines and M-x edit-beginnings-of-lines have no keys.
